Peppermint oil lowers blood pressure in just 20 days

2026-08-23 · J. Whitfield

Peppermint oil may have an unexpected benefit for people with mildly high blood pressure. Adults who took a small dose twice daily for 20 days saw their systolic blood pressure fall by an average of 8.5 mmHg, while a placebo group showed little change.

Source noted: ScienceDaily Health. This write-up is independent commentary and summary.
